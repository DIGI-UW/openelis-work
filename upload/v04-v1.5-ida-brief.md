Hi Ida,

We're locking the V-04 v1.5 Manual Entry Helper spec and have ~8 open assumptions that depend on what you see in SILANTOR day-to-day. ~10 questions. Most are short. Where it helps, I've explained why we're asking.

The short version of where we landed: instead of building a CSV exporter, v1.5 ships a "typing accelerator" screen — large-font Tiles of this week's numbers, each with a Copy button, so your team can paste them into the SILANTOR browser tab without retyping. There's a "Mark week submitted" button at the end that writes an audit row. Admin field-map page lets deployments re-order/relabel metrics without code changes. All the chassis is locked; the specifics below are not, and depend on what you see in the portal.

---

## Cluster A — SILANTOR portal shape

1. **Can you share one or two screenshots of the SILANTOR submission screens your team uses weekly?** (Helps us match field labels exactly, including ordering on the screen — the Tile order on our helper should mirror the portal order so your team copies top-to-bottom.)

2. **Is the data your team enters into SILANTOR (a) vector-control program data (HI/CI/BI, fogging events, ABJ alerts), (b) pool-level pathogen test results (Pools tested, Pools positive, MIR, sporozoite rate), or (c) both, on different screens?** This is the biggest open question for us — it affects whether the helper pulls numbers from the V-04 dashboard (pathogen results path) or from the larval-survey/vector-control workflow (which we haven't built yet). If SILANTOR is (a)-only, the eight default Tiles we've sketched are the wrong eight.

3. **Does SILANTOR accept Sporozoite Rate at all? If yes, in what unit and on what screen?** We auto-gate the Sporozoite Rate Tile when deconvolution coverage is below 95%, but if SILANTOR doesn't ask for sporozoite rate at all, the Tile is dead weight.

4. **What's the portal cadence — weekly, daily during outbreaks, monthly, or something else?** Our spec assumes weekly throughout. If it's daily during outbreaks we may need a different surface, or at least a way to toggle the helper between cadences.

---

## Cluster B — Cadence + workflow

5. **Who actually types into SILANTOR — lab tech, surveillance coordinator, dedicated data clerk?** Affects what role we default the `vectorReport.manualEntryHelper` permission to.

6. **Do they have OpenELIS open in another tab while they type, or do they print/copy notes first?** If it's "print first," the Copy buttons aren't useful and we should lean harder on the Print Laporan Hasil PDF dropdown. If it's "OpenELIS in tab 2," Copy is the right primary action.

7. **When SILANTOR is down or the deadline is missed, what's the recovery flow?** Affects whether we need a queue / retry / late-submission tagging, or whether the audit row is enough.

   - (a) typing speed
   - (b) missed deadlines / reminders
   - (c) numbers in OpenELIS not matching what's in SILANTOR after entry
   - (d) hunting for the right numbers
   - (e) something else

9. **If we could solve only ONE of those this quarter, which would have the most impact?**
