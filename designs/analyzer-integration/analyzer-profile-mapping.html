The reusable setups behind your analyzers.
ℹ️ What is this? An analyzer type is the reusable setup for a kind of analyzer — its test codes, the results it reports and how they map to your tests, formatting, and QC codes. How to use it: identical analyzers share one type, so fixing it once updates them all; when a unit differs, open it and Save as a new type. Analyzer types are deactivated (not deleted) to keep this list tidy.
